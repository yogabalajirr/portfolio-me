Strategy → PRD

Live EV Charger App for India

A pitch PRD for an app that tells India's EV drivers whether a charger actually works — not just where it is. Frames the opportunity (10M EVs, roughly 18% of chargers non-operational), designs a cross-operator reliability layer that blends operator feeds with one-tap driver check-ins, models four revenue streams, and sets explicit kill criteria at a six-week gate.

A lightweight customer-support ticketing tool for 5–50 agent teams, positioned as a faster, cheaper alternative to Zendesk and Freshdesk. Covers market positioning, a tightly scoped MVP (10 features shipped, 12 deliberately deferred to v2), success metrics, risk analysis, and clear non-goals.

Case Study

Offline-First Architecture for WeeFit

Users were losing meal logs to spotty gym and travel connectivity, so I designed a dual-mode Supabase + localStorage architecture with an automatic sync queue. The goal: meal logging keeps working with no connection and takes under 30 seconds, so network latency is never a source of friction. (These are design targets from the case study, not production measurements.)
